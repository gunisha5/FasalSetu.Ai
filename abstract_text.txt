         FASALSETU AI: BRIDGING FARMERS & INSURANCE                           
                         WITH SATELLITE INTELLIGENCE     
 
 
Abstract 
Agricultural insurance in countries like India faces persistent challenges due to manual field 
inspections, delayed claim settlements, and susceptibility to human bias or corruption. These 
inefficiencies reduce farmer trust and hinder timely financial relief during climate-induced crop 
failures such as droughts and floods. This project proposes an AI -driven, satellite-based crop 
damage assessment system that automates insurance claim validation using multi -spectral 
remote sensing data. 
The system leverages imagery from Sentinel-2, which provides high-resolution, multi-spectral 
data across visible, near-infrared (NIR), and shortwave infrared (SWIR) bands. These spectral 
bands enable precise monitoring of vegetation health, soil moisture, and water stress—critical 
indicators of crop damage. 
The architecture consists of three primary components: 
Geospatial Data Acquisition & Preprocessing: 
Satellite imagery is collected periodically for insured farmland regions. Preprocessing includes 
cloud masking, atmospheric correction, and spatial alignment. Vegetation indices such as 
NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) and NDWI (Normalized Dif ference Water 
Index) are computed to quantify plant health and water content. Temporal stacking of these 
indices enables detection of abnormal deviations from baseline crop conditions. 
Damage Detection using Computer Vision & ML: 
A hybrid deep learning pipeline is employed, combining Convolutional Neural Networks 
(CNNs) with temporal models (LSTM/Transformers) to analyze both spatial and time -series 
patterns. The model classifies regions into categories such as healthy, drought-affected, flood-
damaged, or partially impacted. Change detection algorithms further identify sudden 
degradation events, improving claim validation accuracy. 
Insurance Decision Engine & Dashboard: 
A rule-based + ML-driven scoring system converts model outputs into damage severity scores 
aligned with insurance policies. These insights are visualized via a geospatial dashboard for 
insurers and government agencies, enabling automated, transparent, and auditable claim 
processing. Integration with APIs allows seamless linkage with digital agriculture and 
insurance platforms. 
The system is designed to operate at scale, enabling near real -time monitoring of large 
agricultural regions without the need for on-ground inspections. 
 
VETS Evaluation: 
Viability: 
Freely available satellite datasets from European Space Agency (Sentinel program) make this 
solution cost -effective. Cloud platforms like Google Earth Engine can support large -scale 
geospatial processing, while deployment can be done via web dashboards for  insurers and 
government bodies. 
Engineering Depth: 
The project involves advanced Geospatial Data Science, including multi -spectral image 
processing, vegetation index computation, spatio-temporal deep learning, and large-scale data 
pipelines. Handling noisy satellite data (cloud cover, seasonal variation) a nd ensuring model 
generalization across crop types adds significant complexity. 
Trend Alignment: 
Strongly aligned with global trends in AgriTech, Climate AI, and Remote Sensing Analytics. 
Governments and insurance providers are increasingly adopting AI -driven parametric 
insurance models, making this solution highly relevant for 2026 and beyond. 
Social & Sustainability Impact: 
The system directly benefits farmers by enabling faster, fair, and transparent insurance claim 
settlements, reducing financial distress during crop failures. It supports climate resilience, 
promotes sustainable agriculture practices, and minimizes resource  wastage associated with 
manual surveys.
